Changes Required is not an account punishment or suspension. Your profile stays hidden until the required information is corrected and reviewed again.

What happens next?

  1. KaiBandham admin reviews your profile information.
  2. The admin checks that required information is complete and that profile content follows KaiBandham rules.
  3. If everything is ready, your profile is approved.
  4. If something needs correction, your status becomes Changes Required.
  5. You receive a visual notification when the status changes.
Important: Profile approval means the profile passed KaiBandham's review process. It does not mean KaiBandham guarantees that every personal detail provided by a member is true or accurate.
