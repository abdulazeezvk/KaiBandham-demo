KaiBandham Safety

Stay Safe While Finding Your Life Partner

Protect your personal information, communicate carefully and report serious safety concerns.

Your Safety and Privacy Matter

KaiBandham is designed with text-first communication, privacy controls, blocking and reporting tools.

Always use your own judgement when communicating with another member. Profile approval does not mean KaiBandham guarantees every personal detail provided by a member.

🔒

Protect Personal Information

Do not share sensitive personal information too quickly.

  • Keep your personal WhatsApp number private until you choose to approve sharing it.
  • Never share passwords, OTP codes or login information.
  • Avoid sharing bank account, card or financial information.
  • Use KaiBandham privacy controls for photos, email, family contact and profile visibility.
👤

Watch for Fake Profiles

Be careful when profile information appears false, misleading or inconsistent.

  • Check profile information carefully.
  • Be cautious if important details repeatedly change or do not make sense.
  • Be careful with suspicious or misleading photographs.
  • Use Report Profile if you believe a profile is fake.
💰

Never Send Money

Be very careful if another member asks you for money.

  • Do not send money to someone you only know through the internet.
  • Be suspicious of urgent or emotional requests for money.
  • Never share debit card, credit card or banking credentials.
  • Report possible scams or fraud using Report Profile.
💬

Communicate Safely

KaiBandham provides text communication before personal WhatsApp sharing.

  • Members can start KaiBandham messaging only after an interest has been accepted.
  • Each conversation is limited to 15 total messages between both members.
  • After the 15-message limit, a member may request the other person's Personal WhatsApp.
  • Personal WhatsApp is never revealed automatically.
  • The profile owner must explicitly approve the WhatsApp request before their number is shared.
👪

Family Contact

KaiBandham allows a separate family contact for marriage-related communication.

  • Confirm that your family contact information is accurate.
  • Family members should also avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or financial information.
  • Family contact visibility depends on the member's privacy setting.
  • Family Contact and Personal WhatsApp are separate.
🚫

Block Contact

Block Contact is a personal privacy control.

  • A blocked member cannot send you new interests.
  • A blocked member cannot send you new messages.
  • A blocked member cannot send you WhatsApp contact requests.
  • Blocking does not report the member to an administrator.
  • Blocking does not give the member a warning, penalty or suspension.

Block Contact or Report Profile?

🚫 Block Contact

Use Block Contact when you simply do not want communication from someone.

  • Personal privacy choice
  • Stops communication
  • Does not report the member
  • Does not punish the member
  • Can later be removed

⚠️ Report Profile

Use Report Profile for serious safety or rule concerns.

  • Fake profile
  • Scam or fraud
  • Harassment or abuse
  • Inappropriate content
  • False information
Important: A report does not automatically mean that the reported member did something wrong. KaiBandham administrators review reports before deciding whether any action is appropriate.

Report a Profile

Consider reporting a member when you notice a serious concern.

  • Fake profile
  • Scam or fraud
  • Harassment
  • Threatening behaviour
  • Inappropriate photos or content
  • False information
  • Abusive behaviour
  • Other serious safety concerns

What happens after reporting?

Your report starts as Pending , may move to Under Review , and finally becomes Resolved . Admin action is taken only when appropriate after review.

Personal WhatsApp Privacy

Your Personal WhatsApp number is private by default.

15 messages does not automatically reveal your WhatsApp number.
  1. Two members accept an interest.
  2. KaiBandham text messaging becomes available.
  3. The conversation can contain up to 15 total messages between both members.
  4. After reaching the limit, a member may send a WhatsApp contact request.
  5. The other member chooses Approve or Decline.
  6. Personal WhatsApp is shared only after explicit approval.

You can also disable WhatsApp contact requests from your privacy settings.

🤝

Meeting Someone in Person

If you decide to meet a potential marriage partner:

  • Tell your family or another trusted person where you are going.
  • Meet in a public place.
  • Avoid travelling alone to an unfamiliar private location.
  • Bring a family member or trusted person when appropriate.
  • Arrange your own safe transportation where possible.
  • Do not feel pressured to stay if you feel uncomfortable.

Immediate Danger or Emergency

KaiBandham reporting and support tools are not emergency services.

If you believe you or another person is in immediate danger, move to a safe place when possible and contact the appropriate local emergency service, police or a trusted person.
