KaiBandham Rules

Terms of Use

These rules help keep KaiBandham safe, respectful and useful for members looking for marriage partners.

Last updated: September 2026

Development Note: These Terms are currently part of the KaiBandham project design. Before the public website launches, they should be reviewed so they match the final Django system, business operation and applicable legal requirements.

1. About KaiBandham

KaiBandham is an accessible matrimony platform designed for Deaf and hearing-impaired adults in Kerala.

The platform focuses on text-first communication, accessibility, privacy and respectful marriage connections.

2. Age Requirement

Users must be at least 18 years old to create a KaiBandham account.

Anyone under 18 years old must not register or create a KaiBandham matrimony profile.

3. Genuine and Accurate Profiles

Members must provide genuine, accurate and reasonably up-to-date profile information.

  • Do not create fake profiles.
  • Do not impersonate another person.
  • Do not use another person's photograph without permission.
  • Do not intentionally provide false age, religion, marital status, location, education or occupation information.
  • Do not intentionally provide false family or contact information.

4. New Profile Approval

New KaiBandham profiles may remain hidden until they are reviewed by an administrator.

After review, a profile may receive:

  • Approved
  • Changes Required

If Changes Required is shown, the member may be asked to correct or complete information before the profile is reviewed again.

Changes Required is not an account suspension or punishment.

5. Profile Approval Is Not a Guarantee

Profile approval means that the profile passed KaiBandham's administrative review process.

KaiBandham cannot guarantee that every personal detail supplied by every member is true, complete or accurate.

Members should use their own judgement when communicating, verifying information and deciding whether to meet another person.

6. Respectful Behaviour

Members must communicate with other users respectfully.

  • No harassment
  • No abusive language
  • No threats
  • No stalking
  • No unwanted repeated contact
  • No inappropriate sexual content
  • No hateful or discriminatory behaviour
  • No deliberate attempts to intimidate or pressure another member

7. Marriage Interests

Members may send marriage interests to profiles they are interested in.

The receiving member may accept or reject an interest.

Rejecting an interest does not mean the sender has done anything wrong.

Rejection of an interest does not create a warning, report or penalty.

8. Messaging

KaiBandham text messaging becomes available only after an interest has been accepted.

Each accepted conversation may contain a maximum of 15 total messages between both members.

This means the 15-message limit applies to the whole conversation, not 15 messages for each person.

Members must not use messaging to harass, threaten, scam, pressure or abuse another person.

9. Personal WhatsApp Number

Personal WhatsApp numbers are hidden from other members by default.

Reaching the 15-message conversation limit does not automatically reveal either member's WhatsApp number.

After the limit is reached, a member may request the other person's Personal WhatsApp number.

The profile owner must explicitly approve the request before their Personal WhatsApp number is shared.

If the request is declined, the number remains hidden.

10. Family Contact

Members may provide a separate Family Contact for marriage-related communication.

Family Contact is separate from Personal WhatsApp.

Family Contact visibility depends on the profile owner's privacy settings.

Members should provide family contact information only when they have the right or permission to do so.

11. Privacy and Personal Information

Members must respect the privacy and personal information of other KaiBandham users.

  • Do not share another member's phone number without permission.
  • Do not share private profile photographs without permission.
  • Do not publish private conversations without permission.
  • Do not misuse another person's email address or family contact.
  • Do not collect or use member information for scams, advertising or unrelated purposes.

Members should review the Privacy Policy for more information about KaiBandham privacy controls.

12. Scams, Fraud and Money Requests

KaiBandham must not be used for financial scams, fraud or deceptive money requests.

  • Do not ask members for money through false stories or pressure.
  • Do not request banking passwords.
  • Do not request OTP codes.
  • Do not request debit-card or credit-card security information.
  • Do not promote fraudulent schemes.
  • Do not impersonate a business, government service or other person.

13. Block Contact

Members may use Block Contact when they do not want communication from another member.

A blocked member should not be able to send the blocking member:

  • New interests
  • New messages
  • WhatsApp contact requests

Blocking is a personal privacy choice.

Blocking another member does not:

  • Report them to an administrator
  • Give them an account warning
  • Suspend their account
  • Damage their account reputation

14. Reporting a Profile

Report Profile should be used for serious safety or rule concerns.

Reasons may include:

  • Fake profile
  • Scam or fraud
  • Harassment
  • Abuse
  • Threatening behaviour
  • Inappropriate content
  • False information

Reports may move through: Pending → Under Review → Resolved .

A report does not automatically mean the reported member has broken KaiBandham rules.

Reports do not automatically suspend, warn or remove an account.

15. Support Complaints

Support requests are different from safety reports.

Contact KaiBandham Support for problems involving:

  • Your account
  • Your profile
  • Privacy settings
  • Interests
  • Messages
  • WhatsApp contact requests
  • Technical problems

Support complaints may move through: Pending → Under Review → Resolved .

16. Account Warnings and Administrative Action

KaiBandham administrators may take appropriate action when a serious rule violation is confirmed.

Possible administrative actions may include:

  • Warning the member
  • Suspending the account
  • Removing the account

Administrative action should be based on review of the available information and should not occur automatically simply because someone submitted a report.

17. Account Suspension

KaiBandham may suspend an account when a serious violation is confirmed or when suspension is otherwise reasonably necessary to protect the service or members.

Examples may include:

  • Confirmed fake profiles
  • Confirmed scams or fraud
  • Confirmed harassment
  • Serious inappropriate behaviour
  • Serious Terms violations

An administrator should record the reason for a suspension.

18. Account Removal

Serious or confirmed violations may result in permanent account removal.

Fake, fraudulent, abusive or seriously inappropriate accounts may also be removed after administrator review.

Account removal should have a recorded administrative reason.

19. Meeting Another Member

KaiBandham helps members make online marriage connections, but members are responsible for their own decisions about meeting another person.

When meeting someone in person:

  • Meet in a public place
  • Tell a family member or trusted person where you are going
  • Consider bringing a family member or trusted person
  • Avoid unfamiliar private locations when possible
  • Leave if you feel unsafe or uncomfortable

More guidance is available on the Safety page .

20. Service Availability

KaiBandham may occasionally experience maintenance, technical problems or temporary service interruptions.

Features may also be changed, improved or removed as the platform develops.

21. No Guarantee of Marriage or Compatibility

KaiBandham provides a platform for members to discover and communicate with potential marriage partners.

KaiBandham does not guarantee:

  • A successful marriage
  • A successful relationship
  • Compatibility between members
  • That every profile statement is accurate
  • That every member will behave appropriately

Members should make their own decisions and carry out appropriate personal and family verification.

22. Member Account Responsibility

Members are responsible for protecting their account.

  • Keep your password private.
  • Never share your OTP code.
  • Do not allow another person to misuse your account.
  • Contact support if you believe your account has been compromised.

23. Account Deletion

Members should be able to request deletion of their own KaiBandham account.

Account deletion may be subject to necessary data-retention, safety, administrative or legal requirements described in the Privacy Policy.

24. Administrator Responsibilities and Rights

Authorized KaiBandham administrators may review profiles, reports, complaints and relevant account information when necessary to operate the service.

Administrators may also take reasonable action to protect members, enforce these Terms and investigate suspected abuse, scams or fraudulent activity.

25. Changes to These Terms

KaiBandham may update these Terms when features, policies, safety rules or applicable requirements change.

Important changes should be communicated through a visible website notice or member notification.

The Last Updated date at the top of this page should be changed when material changes are made.

26. Contact Support

If you have questions about these Terms or need help with your KaiBandham account, contact support using written communication.
