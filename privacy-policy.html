KaiBandham Privacy

Privacy Policy

Learn what information KaiBandham may collect, how it is used and what privacy controls are available.

Last updated: September 2026

Development Note: This Privacy Policy is currently part of the KaiBandham project design. Before the public website launches, the policy should be reviewed and updated so it accurately matches the final Django system, database, hosting setup and applicable legal requirements.

1. About KaiBandham

KaiBandham is an accessible matrimony platform designed for Deaf and hearing-impaired adults in Kerala.

The platform focuses on text-first communication, privacy controls, accessibility and safe marriage connections.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected, why the information is used and the privacy choices available to members.

2. Information We May Collect

When you register or create a KaiBandham matrimony profile, you may provide information such as:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth and age
  • Gender
  • Religion
  • Marital status
  • Kerala district
  • Town or city
  • Education
  • Occupation
  • Communication Method
  • Preferred Communication
  • Profile photographs
  • About Me information
  • Partner preferences
  • Personal WhatsApp number
  • Family contact name, relationship and number
  • Email address

3. Communication Method

Members may provide their normal Communication Method.

Options may include:

  • Indian Sign Language (ISL)
  • Speech / Spoken Language
  • Sign Language & Speech
  • Other

This is separate from Preferred Communication, which describes how a member prefers other KaiBandham members to contact them.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Create your matrimony profile.
  • Display profile information according to your privacy settings.
  • Help members search for suitable profiles.
  • Process interest requests.
  • Provide KaiBandham text messaging.
  • Provide visual notifications.
  • Process WhatsApp contact requests and approvals.
  • Manage privacy settings.
  • Provide account support.
  • Review safety reports.
  • Review support complaints.
  • Detect or investigate suspicious activity.
  • Maintain the safety and operation of KaiBandham.

5. Personal WhatsApp Privacy

A member's Personal WhatsApp number is hidden from other members by default.

Personal WhatsApp is not automatically revealed because two members have accepted an interest.

After an accepted conversation reaches the KaiBandham limit of 15 total messages between both members, a member may request the other person's Personal WhatsApp number.

The number remains hidden until the profile owner explicitly approves the WhatsApp contact request.

If the owner declines the request, the Personal WhatsApp number remains hidden.

Members may also disable WhatsApp contact requests through their privacy settings.

6. Family Contact Information

Family Contact is separate from Personal WhatsApp.

Members may provide a family contact name, relationship and phone number for marriage-related family communication.

Family Contact visibility may be controlled using privacy settings.

Options may include:

  • Logged-in KaiBandham Members
  • Accepted Interests Only
  • Hidden

The default KaiBandham design currently allows Family Contact visibility to logged-in members, unless the profile owner changes the setting.

7. Email Privacy

A member's email address is hidden from other members by default.

Depending on privacy settings, a member may choose to make their email available to accepted interests.

Email may also be used for account access, password recovery, important account notices and support communication.

8. Profile Photo Privacy

Members can control who may see their profile photographs.

Available options may include:

  • Everyone
  • Accepted Interests Only
  • Nobody

KaiBandham currently uses Accepted Interests Only as the intended default photo privacy option.

9. Profile Visibility

Members may temporarily hide their KaiBandham profile.

When a profile is hidden, it should not appear normally to other members in profile search.

Hiding a profile does not automatically delete the member's account or profile data.

10. Interests and Messages

Members may send marriage interests to other profiles.

KaiBandham text messaging becomes available only after an interest has been accepted.

Each accepted conversation is designed to allow a maximum of 15 total messages between both members.

After the limit is reached, Personal WhatsApp may be requested, but the number is not automatically revealed.

The WhatsApp owner must approve the request first.

11. Blocked Contacts

Block Contact is a personal privacy control.

When you block a member:

  • They cannot send you new interests.
  • They cannot send you new messages.
  • They cannot send you WhatsApp contact requests.

Blocking another member does not automatically create a safety report.

Blocking also does not automatically create an account warning, penalty or suspension for the blocked member.

Blocking information may need to be stored so KaiBandham can enforce the contact restriction.

12. Safety Reports

Members may submit a Report Profile request for serious concerns such as:

  • Fake profiles
  • Scam or fraud
  • Harassment
  • Abuse
  • Inappropriate content
  • False information

Report information may include:

  • Report ID
  • Profile ID of the member submitting the report
  • Reported Profile ID
  • Reason for the report
  • Written report details
  • Date submitted
  • Report status
  • Administrator review information

Reports may move through: Pending → Under Review → Resolved .

Submitting a report does not automatically mean the reported member did something wrong.

Reports do not automatically suspend or remove an account. An administrator reviews the report before deciding whether action is appropriate.

13. Support Complaints

Support complaints are separate from safety reports.

Members may contact KaiBandham Support regarding issues such as:

  • Account problems
  • Profile problems
  • Privacy settings
  • Message problems
  • Interest problems
  • WhatsApp contact problems
  • Technical problems

A support request may store information such as the member's Profile ID, category, subject, message, optional Related Profile ID, preferred reply method and status.

Support requests may move through: Pending → Under Review → Resolved .

14. Administrator Access

Authorized KaiBandham administrators may need access to relevant account or profile information for legitimate administration purposes.

This may include:

  • Reviewing new profiles
  • Reviewing profile changes
  • Investigating safety reports
  • Managing support complaints
  • Responding to member support
  • Investigating fake accounts
  • Investigating scams or harassment
  • Applying account actions when justified after review

Serious administrator actions, such as suspension or removal, should have a recorded reason and administrative record.

15. Notifications

KaiBandham may use visual notifications to tell members about important activity.

Examples may include:

  • New interest requests
  • Accepted interests
  • New messages
  • WhatsApp contact requests
  • WhatsApp request decisions
  • Profile approval status
  • Report status updates
  • Support responses
  • Important administrator notices

16. Your Privacy Controls

KaiBandham members may have controls that allow them to:

  • Keep Personal WhatsApp hidden
  • Allow or disable WhatsApp contact requests
  • Control Family Contact visibility
  • Keep email hidden
  • Control profile photo visibility
  • Temporarily hide their profile
  • Block contacts
  • Unblock contacts
  • Report serious safety concerns
  • Edit profile information
  • Request account deletion

17. Technical Information and Cookies

A production website may collect limited technical information needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

This may include:

  • Browser information
  • Device type
  • Login or session information
  • Security logs
  • Website activity needed for security or troubleshooting

Cookies or similar technologies may also be used where necessary for login sessions, preferences and website functionality.

Before launch, this section must be updated to accurately describe the technologies actually used by the production website.

18. Data Security

The production KaiBandham service should use appropriate technical and administrative measures to help protect member information.

No website or online system can guarantee absolute security.

Members should protect their own account credentials and should never share passwords or OTP codes with another person.

19. Data Retention

KaiBandham should retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for operating the service, safety, account administration and applicable requirements.

Some information may need to be retained after account deletion, for example where necessary for safety records, abuse prevention, legal obligations or administrative audit records.

The final production policy must explain actual retention periods when they have been decided.

20. Account Deletion

Members should be able to request deletion of their KaiBandham account.

Before public launch, KaiBandham should clearly explain:

  • How deletion can be requested
  • Which information is deleted
  • Whether some information must be retained
  • How long account deletion normally takes

21. Age Requirement

KaiBandham is intended for adults aged 18 years or older who are legally eligible to use a matrimony service.

People under 18 years of age should not create a KaiBandham account.

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

KaiBandham may update this Privacy Policy when website features, privacy controls, technology or applicable requirements change.

Important changes should be communicated through a visible website notice or member notification.

The Last Updated date at the top of this page should also be changed when the policy is materially updated.

23. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have a question about privacy, personal information, your account or a data request, contact KaiBandham Support using written communication.
