How KaiBandham Works

From creating your profile to communicating with an accepted interest.

3

Send a marriage interest to someone you would like to know better.

Text Chat

KaiBandham messaging becomes available after the interest is accepted.

6

Request WhatsApp

After 15 total chat messages, a member may request the other person's Personal WhatsApp.

Profile Review: A new profile may be Approved or marked Changes Required . Changes Required is not an account punishment.

Privacy and Safety

Blocking and reporting are two different KaiBandham tools.

🚫

Block Contact

Use Block Contact when you do not want another member to contact you.

Blocking does not report, warn, suspend or punish the other member.

⚠️

Report Profile

Use Report Profile for serious concerns such as fake profiles, scams, harassment or abuse.

Reports are reviewed by an administrator before account action is taken.

Made for Kerala

KaiBandham currently focuses on Deaf and hearing-impaired matrimony connections within Kerala.

Important

KaiBandham helps members discover and communicate with potential marriage partners. Profile approval does not guarantee that every personal detail supplied by another member is accurate. Members and families should use appropriate judgement and verification before making marriage decisions.
