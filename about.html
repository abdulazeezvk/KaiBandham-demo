About KaiBandham

Accessible Matrimony for Deaf People Across Kerala

KaiBandham is a matrimony platform designed for Deaf and hearing-impaired adults in Kerala to find suitable marriage partners and communicate through accessible, text-first tools.

Create Account Search Profiles

Why KaiBandham?

Many traditional matrimony platforms are not designed around the communication needs of Deaf and hearing-impaired people.

KaiBandham focuses on accessible, text-first communication and gives members more control over privacy, contact information and communication.

KaiBandham currently focuses on Kerala, allowing members to search by district, age, religion, marital status, Communication Method, education, occupation and other marriage preferences.

Who Is KaiBandham For?

KaiBandham is designed for adults aged 18 years or older who are looking for marriage connections within Kerala.

Members can describe their normal Communication Method using options such as:

  • Indian Sign Language (ISL)
  • Speech / Spoken Language
  • Sign Language & Speech
  • Other

Communication Method describes how a member normally communicates. Preferred Communication is separate and describes how they prefer other KaiBandham members to contact them.

KaiBandham currently focuses on matrimony connections within Kerala.

Members can choose their Kerala district and town or city when creating a profile.

Text Chat and Personal WhatsApp

KaiBandham messaging becomes available only after two members have an accepted interest.

Each conversation can contain a maximum of 15 total messages between both members.

Important: Reaching 15 messages does not automatically reveal a WhatsApp number.

After the 15-message limit, one member may send a Personal WhatsApp request.

The WhatsApp number remains hidden until the profile owner explicitly approves the request.

Personal Contact and Family Contact

KaiBandham separates a member's Personal WhatsApp from Family Contact.

Personal WhatsApp is intended for the member's own communication and remains hidden unless the owner approves sharing it.

A separate Family Contact can be provided for a father, mother, brother, sister, guardian or another relative.

Family Contact visibility depends on the profile owner's privacy setting.

Profile Review

New profiles may be reviewed by a KaiBandham administrator before they become publicly visible.

A new profile can have one of these states:

  • Pending Review
  • Approved
  • Changes Required

Changes Required means that some profile information needs to be corrected or completed.

It is not an account punishment or suspension.

Privacy and Safety

KaiBandham includes separate tools for personal privacy and serious safety concerns.

🚫 Block Contact

Blocking stops unwanted interests, messages and WhatsApp contact requests. Blocking is a personal privacy action and does not punish or report the other member.

⚠️ Report Profile

Reporting is for serious concerns such as fake profiles, scams, harassment, abuse or inappropriate content. Reports are reviewed by administrators before action is taken.

Read Safety Guidance

Accessibility

KaiBandham is designed around visual and text-based interaction.

  • Text-first communication
  • Visual notifications
  • Clear buttons and navigation
  • Keyboard-accessible controls
  • Visible focus and skip links
  • No required voice calls

KaiBandham does not require users to make voice calls in order to use the main matrimony features.

Important

KaiBandham helps members discover and communicate with potential marriage partners.

Profile approval does not guarantee that every personal detail provided by another member is accurate. Members and families should use their own judgement and appropriate verification before making marriage decisions.
