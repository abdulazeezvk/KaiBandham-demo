About Blocking

Blocking is a personal privacy control. It does not mean the other member has done anything wrong.

A blocked member does not receive an account warning, penalty, reputation loss or suspension. Blocking also does not create an administrator report.

The number of people who block a member will never automatically suspend, remove or punish that member. If there is a serious safety problem, use Report Profile separately.

What Happens When You Block Contact?

  • They cannot send you new interests.
  • They cannot send you new messages.
  • They cannot send you Personal WhatsApp requests.
  • They cannot continue normal member-to-member contact with you while the block is active.
  • Blocking does not automatically report them to an administrator.
  • Blocking does not damage or penalize their KaiBandham account.

What happens if I unblock someone?

Unblocking removes your personal contact block. The member can interact with you again according to normal KaiBandham rules, privacy settings and interest requirements.

Unblocking does not automatically restore an old interest, conversation, message history access or previous WhatsApp approval. A new interaction must follow the normal KaiBandham rules.

Block Contact vs Report Profile

Block Contact is for your personal privacy. It stops communication between you and the member.

Report Profile is for serious safety concerns such as fake profiles, scams, harassment, false information or inappropriate content.

Reporting creates a separate safety report for administrator review. A report also does not automatically suspend or punish the member. An administrator reviews the report before deciding whether action is needed.
