Control who can see your personal information and contact details.

Contact Information

Your personal WhatsApp number and email are private by default. Your family contact number is visible to logged-in KaiBandham members by default, but you can change that below.

Personal WhatsApp Number

Your personal WhatsApp number is not shown automatically. After 15 total chat messages, an accepted member may request your WhatsApp number. You decide whether to approve the request.

Choose who can see the separate family contact number. This number may be used for communication with a family member or guardian.

Messages & Contact Requests

Allow Messages

Only members whose interest you have accepted can message you.

Allow WhatsApp Requests

After a conversation reaches 15 total messages, the other member can request your personal WhatsApp number. Your number will remain hidden until you approve the request.

Contact Sharing: Reaching 15 messages does not automatically reveal your personal WhatsApp number. You must approve the request first.

Profile Visibility

Show My Profile

Turn this off if you want to temporarily hide your profile from search results. Your account will not be deleted.

Blocked Contacts

Blocking is a personal privacy control. It does not mean another member has done anything wrong.

What does blocking do?
  • Stops that member from sending you new interests.
  • Stops that member from sending you messages.
  • Stops WhatsApp contact requests from that member.
  • Does not report the member to KaiBandham admin.
  • Does not give the member a warning or account penalty.
