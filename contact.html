What happens after you submit?

  1. Your support request is created.
  2. Its first status is Pending.
  3. An administrator reviews it.
  4. The status can change to Under Review.
  5. You receive a response when support has more information.
  6. When the issue is finished, the request becomes Resolved.

Reporting Another Member

Support requests and safety reports are different. If another member is fake, attempting a scam, harassing you, threatening you or posting inappropriate content, open that member's profile and use Report Profile .

Reporting sends the concern to administrator review. It does not automatically suspend or punish the reported member.
