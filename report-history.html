Report History

View safety reports you have submitted to KaiBandham administrators and check their current status.

How Reports Work

Reporting a member does not automatically warn, suspend, remove or otherwise punish their account.

Multiple reports also do not automatically result in punishment. KaiBandham administrators review the available information before deciding whether any action is required.

Report status is managed by KaiBandham administrators. Members cannot change the report status themselves.

Report Privacy

You can see the current status and general outcome of reports you submitted. Private administrator notes, investigation details and another member's private account information are not shown here.

Need to Report Another Member?

Open the member's profile and choose Report Profile for a serious safety concern such as:

  • Fake Profile
  • Scam / Fraud
  • Harassment
  • Inappropriate Photo
  • False Information
  • Inappropriate Behaviour
  • Other serious safety concern
Find Profile

Block Contact and Report Profile Are Different

Block Contact is a personal privacy action. It stops contact but does not create an administrator report or punishment.

Report Profile sends a safety concern to KaiBandham administrators for review.

Need Help With Something Else?

For account, profile, privacy, messages, interests, WhatsApp contact or technical problems, use KaiBandham Support instead of Report Profile.
